Skincare that writes back. For the skin you have, not the skin you are told to want.

The idea

Most skincare tells you what is wrong.
Ours tells you what is right.

Anti-aging. Flawless. Correct, conceal, perfect. The language of beauty often begins with a quiet message: you are not enough yet.

KINDLY YOURS begins somewhere else. With trust. With the calm that comes when you stop fighting your own reflection.

Every product is a short letter from us to you. Gentle, well-chosen ingredients, explained honestly, and a ritual that feels less like repair and more like coming back to yourself.

Letter 01 · Cleansing milk

Dear Morning

The first kind moment of the day.

What’s inside, and what it does for you
OatThe calming one

Rich in natural soothing compounds, it comforts skin that feels tight or sensitive after the night.

ChamomileThe gentle one

Its bisabolol helps quiet redness, so cleansing never feels like a fight.

Rice waterThe softening one

A traditional ingredient that leaves skin smooth and supple, never stripped.

Your ritualMorning and evening. Massage onto dry skin, add a little warm water, rinse slowly.

Read the letterClose the letter

Dear you,

before the day asks anything of you, this moment is yours.

Warm water. Soft hands. The quiet smell of oat and chamomile, like a kitchen where someone has already made tea.

You don’t have to be ready yet. You don’t have to be anything yet.

Just begin gently.

Kindly yours.

Letter 02 · Calming serum

You Can Rest Now

For skin, and minds, that have carried too much.

What’s inside, and what it does for you
Centella asiaticaThe healer

Known for calming irritated skin and supporting it while it recovers.

NiacinamideThe strengthener

Helps reinforce the skin barrier and, with time, evens out tone and texture.

White teaThe protector

Antioxidants that help defend skin against the stress of city air and long days.

Your ritualEvening. Three drops on clean skin, pressed in with your palms. Breathe while you do it.

Read the letterClose the letter

Dear you,

you have been holding so much. The deadlines, the noise, the version of you everyone expects.

Put it down for a while.

Let your skin breathe out, the way you do when you finally close the door behind you. A few drops, a few slow breaths.

Nothing is urgent here.

Kindly yours.

Letter 03 · Barrier cream

Nothing to Fix

No correction. Only care for what is already good.

What’s inside, and what it does for you
CeramidesThe keeper

Lipids your skin makes itself. They seal the barrier so moisture stays where it belongs.

SqualaneThe familiar one

Mirrors your skin’s own oils, so it nourishes without feeling heavy.

Shea butterThe comforter

Rich fatty acids that soften dry patches and leave skin feeling held.

Your ritualAs the last step, morning and evening. Warm a small amount between your fingertips first.

Kindly yours.
