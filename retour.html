the idea

A house of scents built around one quiet feeling: coming home.

To a place, to a person, to yourself. RETOUR is for the moments when the world grows still and something familiar finds you again. Soft notes, intimate words, a world that is slow on purpose.

nº 1

Velvet Dusk

Vanilla · Cashmere wood · A breath of smoke

The hour when the world grows soft.

Read the storyClose the story

The day has finally let go of you.
Outside, the light turns blue and quiet,
and somewhere a fire has just been lit.

You pull the blanket closer.
It still holds the warmth of your skin,
the softness of wool,
the faint smoke of a candle you blew out too late.

Nothing is asked of you now.
No one is waiting.
Only this:
the slow sweetness of vanilla,
the calm of wood that has known many winters,
and the feeling of being exactly where you belong.

Velvet Dusk is the hour when the world grows soft,
and you return to yourself.

nº 2

Skin of Sunday

White musk · Sandalwood · Rice cream

The scent of someone you love, before the day begins.

Read the storyClose the story

No alarm today.
The light comes in slowly, as if it knows not to hurry.

You turn and find the warmth they left behind,
a trace on the pillow,
clean and soft and close,
like the inside of a sweater
worn by someone who loves you.

There is nothing to do,
nowhere to be.
Only the hush of sandalwood,
the milky softness of rice,
and skin that smells like safety.

Skin of Sunday is the closeness you return to,
again and again.

nº 3

Before We Grew Up

Tonka bean · Cinnamon · Warm milk · Fig

For the time when everything was simple.

Read the storyClose the story

Do you remember?
The kitchen in the afternoon,
sunlight falling across the table,
a cup of warm milk with too much cinnamon.

Outside, the fig tree heavy with fruit,
your hands sticky, your knees grass-stained,
no idea yet of what the world would ask of you.

Someone was always there.
Someone always called you in.

Tonka, sweet and round like a lullaby.
Fig, green and golden like summer.

Before We Grew Up is not a memory.
It is a way back.

Some scents don’t arrive.
They return.
