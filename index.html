05The Founder

Behind ANIMA is Ayla.

Fragrance, skin and the quiet psychology of beauty have shaped the way I see the world for as long as I can remember.

I am drawn to what lies beneath the surface: why a scent can bring back an entire summer, why a texture can feel like comfort, why some stories stay with us long after we have heard them.

Books, languages and acting have taught me that every brand, like every person, has a voice of its own. It only needs to be listened to.

ANIMA is where these passions meet: a place where beauty and fragrance houses find a visual and narrative soul that feels truly their own.

Ayla
